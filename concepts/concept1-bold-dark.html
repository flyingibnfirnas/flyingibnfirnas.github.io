About Me

Engineer by Profession,
Builder by Nature

Welcome! I'm Mohamed Helal, known online as Flying Ibn Firnas — named after Abbas ibn Firnas, the 9th-century polymath who dared to fly centuries before anyone thought it possible.


I'm passionate about engineering across multiple domains: from writing firmware for embedded systems and microcontrollers, to working on automotive diagnostics and aerospace systems. I believe the best engineers are the ones who never stop building, learning, and experimenting.


This website hosts my personal and professional projects, things I've built, problems I've solved, and ideas I'm exploring.
