About Me

The story behind Flying Ibn Firnas

I'm Mohamed Helal, an engineer who works across multiple domains — from writing bare-metal firmware for microcontrollers, to building diagnostic tools for vehicles, to dreaming about aerospace systems.


The name Flying Ibn Firnas comes from Abbas ibn Firnas, a 9th-century Andalusian polymath who built wings and attempted flight centuries before the Wright brothers. His fearless spirit of experimentation and cross-disciplinary thinking is what drives my work.
